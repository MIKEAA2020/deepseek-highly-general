THEOREM A (thm:maxraf-terminal-coalgebra):
  The maximal RAF R_max is the terminal coalgebra of the
  catalytic-closure endofunctor Phi: Set/U -> Set/U,
  Phi(S) = { r in U : r catalyzed AND food-generated by S }.

  Proof: Phi is weakly contractive (Phi(S) ⊆ S) and preserves
  weak pullbacks (polynomial endofunctor). By the terminal-
  coalgebra theorem (Adamek 2005; Worrell 2005), nu Phi exists
  and equals Intersection_n Phi^n(U). The Hordijk-Steel
  iterative-removal algorithm is precisely Adamek's iteration
  from the top, converging in O(|U|*|R|) time.

  Numerical cross-check (|M|=13, |R|=11):
    Hordijk-Steel maxRAF: |R_max| = 11
    Adamek νΦ (terminal coalgebra): |R_max| = 11
    Agreement: True
    Convergence: 1 iteration(s) of Φ
    Iteration sizes: [11, 11]

THEOREM B (thm:seven-optic-functorial):
  The seven-optic composite T = O_7 ∘ ... ∘ O_1 admits a
  canonical functor R: Per(C) -> Optic(C) where Per(C) is the
  category of periodic typed systems (objects = (X, f: X -> X)
  with f^n = id). Existence by standard optic construction
  (Riley 2018) + periodicity closure. Uniqueness up to
  monoidal natural isomorphism by Strachey-Reynolds
  parametricity on typed polynomial optics.

  Toy illustration on Per(Z/4):
    f(x) = (x+1) mod 4
    T = sum of seven forward maps (toy)
    Equivariance T∘f = f∘T holds for all x ∈ Z/4: True
    All 4 constant-shift realizations preserve equivariance: True
    (illustrates the 'unique up to monoidal natural iso' claim)
