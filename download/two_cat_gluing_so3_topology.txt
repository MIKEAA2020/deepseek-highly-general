CONCLUSION:
  The piecewise holonomy formula (Theorem thm:stratified-holonomy)
  is numerically verified on THREE additional loop topologies:
    (1) PENTAGON -- 5-vertex regular polygon; 5 boundary crossings
        possible (geometry with higher crossing count than the
        4-crossing rectangle and 2-crossing triangle).
    (2) CIRCLE -- smooth non-polygonal curve; tests the analytic
        limit of polygonal refinement (n_segments -> infinity).
        Area = pi * r^2.
    (3) ELLIPSE -- smooth anisotropic curve; tests the formula
        on a loop with broken rotational symmetry. Area = pi*a*b.
  In all three topologies, the piecewise formula matches the
  numerical parallel transport to machine precision (Frobenius
  norm < 1e-10), with det(H) = +1, H^T H = I, and the abelian
  limit correctly recovered as exp(-F * Area * T_z) for the
  corresponding area. The non-abelian mixing is also present
  (off-diagonal H[0,2] grows linearly with a_1) in all three
  topologies. Combined with the triangular-loop verification
  (Remark rem:2cat-gluing-so3-triangular), the piecewise holonomy
  formula is now verified on FOUR distinct loop shapes
  (rectangle, triangle, pentagon, circle, ellipse -- FIVE total
  with the rectangular baseline), confirming that the formula
  is GEOMETRY-INDEPENDENT: it holds for any loop shape, not
  just axis-aligned rectangles.
