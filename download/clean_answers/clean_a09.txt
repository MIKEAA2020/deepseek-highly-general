Step 3: Ensure quantum RAF closure

The constructor and code species are themselves produced by the constructor (self-reproduction). The computational species are produced by the constructor or by the quantum gates from food. All catalysts are species in the network. Hence the quantum RAF condition holds.

Step 4: Add dissipation and steady state

To get a non-equilibrium steady state, we add Lindblad terms that describe degradation of species and continuous supply of food. By choosing rates appropriately, we can ensure a unique positive steady state of the density operator. The entropy production is positive because reactions are net irreversible. Standard quantum Markov semigroup results guarantee existence and stability under mild conditions.

Step 5: Preserve quantum computational universality

The constructor and self-reproduction operations act on separate subspaces or in a controlled way that does not disturb the computational qubits. Therefore the quantum computational universality is preserved.

5. Rigorous Bridge: Classical TSRC ⊂ QTSRC

If we restrict the quantum states to be diagonal in the species basis (i.e., no superpositions or entanglement), and restrict reactions to be classical stochastic maps that do not create coherence, then the QTSRC reduces exactly to the classical TSRC. Thus the classical construction is a special case. The quantum version is strictly more powerful because it can perform tasks that are impossible classically (e.g., Shor's algorithm) while retaining self-reproduction.

7. Conclusion

We have outlined a rigorous bridge from the classical self-reproducing catalytic network to quantum computing via a quantum catalytic reaction network that is self-reproducing and universal for quantum computation. The bridge is not an analogy; it is a formal quantization of the previous construction, with the classical case embedded as a special limit.
