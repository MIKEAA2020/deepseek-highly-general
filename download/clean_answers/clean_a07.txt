Why this goes beyond RAF

A standard RAF only guarantees that species can be produced. It says nothing about whether the network can 

repair its own reaction topology

 under damage. The RAC condition adds a self-model: the network contains an explicit encoding of its own reactions and uses that encoding to restore damaged catalysts. This is a form of reflexive closure not present in ordinary autocatalytic networks.
