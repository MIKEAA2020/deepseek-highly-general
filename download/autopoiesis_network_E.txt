TASK (b): FULLY AUTOPOIETIC INTEGRATED MR-GR NETWORK (Network E)
       Isozymes + substrate-induced enzyme expression + basal TF +
       backup ATP (ACK) + anaplerotic PEPC
==============================================================================

Network E design (improvements over Network D):
  (1) ISOZYMES: 2 distinct enzymes per metabolic reaction (20 enzymes total)
      -> single-enzyme knockouts don't kill the metabolic pathway
  (2) SUBSTRATE-INDUCED EXPRESSION: each enzyme's synthesis
      requires 1 ALA + 1 ASP + 1 ATP + the substrate of the reaction
      it catalyzes (inducer). Catabolite-repression-like behavior:
      when the substrate is present, the enzyme is synthesized;
      when depleted, synthesis stops.
  (3) BASAL TF SYNTHESIS (G_const, no catalyst): breaks the vicious cycle
      of TF autocatalysis; TF recovers from 0 via constitutive basal rate
  (4) BACKUP ATP (ACK, 2 isozymes): non-glycolytic ATP source from AcCoA;
      ATP regenerates even when glycolysis is partially blocked
  (5) ANAPLEROTIC PEPC (2 isozymes): PEP -> OAA bypass for TCA-cycle OAA
  Total: 39 species (10 food + 29 non-food), 42 reactions

Closed loop: TF (basal) -> enzyme synthesis (substrate-induced) ->
             metabolic reactions (isozyme-catalyzed) -> produces
             ALA, ASP, ATP, AcCoA, OAA -> enzyme synthesis ->
             enzymes catalyze metabolism -> TF autocatalyzes + basal ->
             AcCoA -> ACK -> backup ATP -> all reactions
