INTERPRETATION:
  The full iJO1366 model shows substantially more causally internal
  components than the small 10-species Network B subnetwork. This is
  because genome-scale metabolism has built-in REDUNDANCY: most
  metabolites have multiple producing reactions (isozymes, alternative
  pathways), so knocking out one set of producing reactions leaves
  alternative routes that still produce the metabolite. The system is
  thus more autopoietic at genome scale than at the core-metabolic
  subnetwork scale, where the redundancy is absent.

  However, the system is still NOT fully autopoietic: the components
  that fail the test are those whose production depends on enzyme
  catalysts that are themselves not modeled in the metabolic network.
  The integrated metabolic-gene-regulatory network (Task 3) addresses
  this by adding enzyme-synthesis reactions that close the loop.
