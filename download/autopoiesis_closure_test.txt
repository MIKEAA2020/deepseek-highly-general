  Discussion: The Hordijk-Steel RAF is closed under catalysis but the closure test
  reveals whether the closure is *endogenous* (the system regenerates the knocked-out
  component) or *definitional* (the closure is a property of the static network, not
  the dynamics). The E. coli core-metabolic subnetwork similarly distinguishes
  autopoietic closure (the catalytic machinery regenerates) from homeostatic
  maintenance (the metabolites are maintained but the catalytic machinery does not
  self-regenerate within the modeled network).
