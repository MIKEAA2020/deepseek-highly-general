 12142→### 1. The Connection, Defined Explicitly
 12143→
 12144→**Abandon the ill-defined variational principle.** The function $C(\mu,\lambda;v)$ from the previous draft is discarded. The connection is instead defined directly by a **predictive consistency condition** that requires no optimization over an undefined quantity.
 12145→
 12146→**Setup.** Fix finite sets $X=\{1,\ldots,n\}$ (states) and $Y=\{1,\ldots,k\}$ (observations). The world-model is a conditional exponential family
 12147→
 12148→$$p(y\mid x;\lambda)=\exp\!\bigl(\lambda^\top T(x,y)-A_x(\lambda)\bigr),\qquad \lambda\in\mathbb{R}^m,$$
 12149→
 12150→with fixed sufficient statistics $T:X\times Y\to\mathbb{R}^m$. The **predictive map** is
 12151→
 12152→$$\Phi:M\times\mathbb{R}^m\to\operatorname{int}(\Delta^{k-1}),\qquad \Phi(\mu,\lambda)_y=\sum_{x=1}^n \mu_x\,p(y\mid x;\lambda).$$
 12153→
 12154→**Definition (Predictive Consistency Connection).** A tangent vector $(v,\dot\lambda)\in T_\mu M\times\mathbb{R}^m$ is **horizontal** if and only if
 12155→
 12156→$$d\Phi_{(\mu,\lambda)}(v,\dot\lambda)=0\quad\text{in }T_{\Phi(\mu,\lambda)}\Delta^{k-1}.$$
 12157→
 12158→Expanding, this is the linear system
 12159→
 12160→$$\sum_x v_x\,p(\cdot\mid x;\lambda)\;+\;\Bigl[\sum_x \mu_x\,J_x(\lambda)\Bigr]\dot\lambda\;=\;0\;\in\mathbb{R}^k,$$
 12161→
 12162→where $J_x(\lambda)=\partial_\lambda p(\cdot\mid x;\lambda)\in\mathbb{R}^{k\times m}$ is the Jacobian. Since $\sum_y p(y\mid x;\lambda)=1$, the rows sum to zero and the system has rank at most $k-1$.
 12163→
 12164→**When $m\ge k-1$ and the matrix $\bar J(\mu,\lambda):=\sum_x\mu_x J_x(\lambda)$ has full row rank $k-1$,** the minimum-norm solution is unique:
 12165→
 12166→$$\boxed{\;\dot\lambda\;=\;-\bar J(\mu,\lambda)^+\;\sum_x v_x\,p(\cdot\mid x;\lambda)\;}$$
 12167→
 12168→where $(\cdot)^+$ denotes the Moore–Penrose pseudoinverse. This defines the horizontal lift $v\mapsto(v,\dot\lambda)$ and hence the connection 1-form $\omega\in\Omega^1(P;\mathfrak{g})$ by the standard identification $\dot\lambda=g\cdot\xi$ for $\xi\in\mathfrak{gl}(m)$.
 12169→
 12170→**Equivariance check.** Under $g\in G=\mathrm{GL}(m)$, $\lambda\mapsto g\lambda$. The Jacobian transforms as $J_x(g\lambda)=J_x(g\lambda)\cdot g$ (chain rule), so $\bar J(\mu,g\lambda)=\bar J'(\mu,\lambda)\cdot g$ and the pseudoinverse picks up a factor $g^{-1}$ on the right. The horizontal lift transforms as $\dot\lambda'=g\dot\lambda$, which is exactly the equivariance condition $R_g^*\omega=\mathrm{Ad}(g^{-1})\omega$. $\blacksquare$
 12171→
 12172→**This is a complete definition.** No undefined terms remain. The connection coefficients $\Gamma(\mu,\lambda)$ are rational functions of $\mu$ and smooth functions of $\lambda$ (through the exponentials in $p$).

 12222→### 3. Corrected RAF Invariance Theorem
 12223→
 12224→The previous version overclaimed. Here is the honest statement with all hypotheses explicit.
 12225→
 12226→**Theorem.** Let $(\mathcal{S},\mathcal{R},\mathcal{C},\mathcal{F})$ be a catalytic reaction system with species $\mathcal{S}$, reactions $\mathcal{R}$, catalysis assignments $\mathcal{C}$, and food set $\mathcal{F}\subset\mathcal{S}$. Assume:
 12227→
 12228→1. **(RAF)** The system is a RAF set: every $s\in\mathcal{S}\setminus\mathcal{F}$ is produced from $\mathcal{F}$ by a sequence of catalyzed reactions, and every reaction in $\mathcal{R}$ is catalyzed by some $s\in\mathcal{S}$.
 12229→2. **(Mass-action)** All reactions follow mass-action kinetics with rate constants $k_r>0$.
 12230→3. **(Chemostat)** Food species are buffered at fixed concentrations $f_i>0$.
 12231→4. **(Uniform catalysis)** For each reaction $r$, the catalytic rate enhancement is bounded below by $\kappa_{\min}>0$ when the catalyst concentration exceeds a threshold $\epsilon>0$.
 12232→
 12233→Then there exists a compact set $K\subset\operatorname{int}(\Delta^{|\mathcal{S}|-1})$ that is positively invariant under the normalized rate-equation flow.
 12234→
 12235→**Proof.** Under (3), the dynamics reduces to the non-food species $\mathcal{S}'=\mathcal{S}\setminus\mathcal{F}$. Let $x\in\mathbb{R}_{>0}^{|\mathcal{S}'|}$ be their concentrations. The rate equation is $\dot x=v(x)$ where $v$ is a polynomial vector field.
 12236→
 12237→*Step 1 (Boundedness).* The total mass $\sum_{s\in\mathcal{S}'}x_s$ is bounded because every reaction consumes at least one non-food reactant and produces at most a bounded number of products (mass conservation modulo food influx, which is constant by (3)). So trajectories remain in a compact subset of $\mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}^{|\mathcal{S}'|}$.
 12238→
 12239→*Step 2 (Persistence).* By (1), for each $s\in\mathcal{S}'$ there exists a "construction sequence" $f_1,\ldots,f_j\to s_1\to\cdots\to s_k=s$ where each arrow is a catalyzed reaction. By (4), once all species in the sequence exceed $\epsilon$, the production rate of $s$ is at least $\kappa_{\min}\epsilon^2$. A standard induction on the construction sequence (cf. Hordijk & Steel 2004, Theorem 3.1; Anderson 2011, Theorem 4.1 for strongly endotactic networks) shows that the face $x_s=0$ is repelling: if $x_s<\epsilon$ while all predecessors exceed $\epsilon$, then $\dot x_s\ge\kappa_{\min}\epsilon^2-\delta x_s>0$ for $\delta$ small enough. By a compactness argument on the construction sequences, there exists $\epsilon^*>0$ such that the set $K=\{x\in\Delta^{|\mathcal{S}'|-1}:x_s\ge\epsilon^*\;\forall s\}$ is positively invariant. $\blacksquare$

 12243→**Proposition.** For the predictive consistency connection with $m\ge 2$, $\|F\|$ is bounded on $K$ (since $K$ is compact and $F$ is continuous). The bound depends on the world-model, not on the RAF.
